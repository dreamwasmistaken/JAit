Our Approach

The Apex Method.

01
Diagnostic Assessment
Every student begins with a personalised diagnostic session to map their exact strengths, gaps, and learning style — no guesswork.
02
Targeted Lesson Plans
A custom syllabus is built for each student, focusing effort where it drives maximum grade improvement within the available time.
03
Active Recall Practice
We use spaced repetition and retrieval practice — the most evidence-backed techniques in learning science — to lock in knowledge.
04
Exam Simulation & Review
Weekly past paper sessions under timed conditions, with detailed mark-scheme review so students understand exactly how marks are awarded.
05
Parent Progress Reports
Monthly detailed reports and parent-tutor meetings keep families informed and students accountable — education as a partnership.
